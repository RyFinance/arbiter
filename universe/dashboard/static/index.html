What Arbiter doesGitHub ↗

Every Polymarket binary pays exactly $1 to one side. Arbiter buys one side when it's cheap early in a volatile market, then waits for the other side to get cheap enough that the pair costs less than $1 after fees. It scans the whole market universe, or only the rotating BTC up/down windows.

Dry-runNo key. Live public order books, simulated fills.
PaperWith a key. Authenticated market access, simulated fills.
LiveOpt-in. Real fill-or-kill orders on your account, capped.

How the numbers stay honest
  • Real fills. Every simulated order walks the live order book: it fills only if the full size is there within the limit, at the depth-weighted price.
  • Real fees. Each fill pays Polymarket's taker-fee curve.
  • No fill, no trade. Thin books mean the leg simply doesn't go, the same as a killed market order.
  • Kill switch. A daily loss limit halts new entries; Stop writes an emergency-stop file the bot obeys.
